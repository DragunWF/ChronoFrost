# ChronoFrost: Temporal Augments (Milestone Upgrades)

## System Overview

When the player reaches specific score milestones (e.g., 1000, 2500, 5000 points), the game temporarily pauses, and the player is presented with the **Augment Draft**.

Three "cards" appear on the screen, each offering a distinct, permanent, and randomly selected upgrade. The player can only choose **one** per milestone. This system forces the player to adapt their build dynamically based on what the game offers them.

---

## 1. Offensive Augments (Weapon Upgrades)

These upgrades directly affect the player's primary shooting mechanics. Many of these can be stacked multiple times for compounding effects.

- **Twin-Link Projectors (Double Shot):** Fires an additional bullet parallel to the main shot.
- **Spread Shot:** Adds two angled bullets to the primary shot, creating a shotgun-like cone.
- **Overclocked Barrels (Fire Rate):** Reduces the cooldown between shots by 15%, allowing for a much faster stream of bullets.
- **Tungsten Core (Piercing):** Bullets pass through the first enemy they hit, dealing damage to the enemy directly behind them.
- **Heavy Caliber (Damage/Size):** Increases the size of the player's bullets, making it easier to hit targets and allowing them to shatter larger Ice Cubes faster.
- **Ricochet Rounds:** Bullets that miss enemies will bounce off the screen edges once before disappearing.

## 2. Temporal Augments (Chrono-Freeze Upgrades)

These upgrades enhance the player's core time-manipulation mechanic, making the "Spacebar" ability more forgiving or more powerful.

- **Expanded Capacitor (Max Capacity):** Increases the maximum size of the Chrono-Charge meter by 25%.
- **Optimized Coolant (Slower Drain):** The Chrono-Charge meter depletes 15% slower while time is frozen.
- **Absolute Zero (Deeper Freeze):** Increases the intensity of the time freeze (e.g., shifts the time scale from `0.15` down to `0.05`), bringing enemies closer to a complete halt.
- **Ember Efficiency:** Every Thermal Ember picked up restores 20% more energy to the Chrono-Charge meter.

## 3. Utility & Survival Augments

Quality-of-life improvements and defensive buffs that help the player survive the bullet hell.

- **Magnetic Field:** Increases the pickup radius for Thermal Embers and Powerups, pulling them toward the player from further away.
- **Agility Thrusters:** Increases the player's base WASD movement speed by 10%.
- **Kinetic Plating:** Grants +1 Max Life. (Can be made a rare card).
- **Shatter Retaliation:** When the player takes damage and loses a life, a small ring of bullets is automatically fired outward in all directions.
- **Smaller Hitbox:** Reduces the physical size of the player's core, making it easier to slip through tight gaps in enemy bullet patterns.
